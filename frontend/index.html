What is the minimum order amount?
There is no minimum order amount. However, orders above ₹499 qualify for free delivery. Below that, a flat ₹40 delivery fee applies.
How long does delivery take?
Our average delivery time is 25–30 minutes. For late-night orders or peak hours, it may take up to 40 minutes. You can track your order in real-time after placing it.
Are there vegan or gluten-free options?
Yes! We have several plant-based options including our Veg Portobello Burger and Paneer Tikka Burger. Filter by 'Veg Only' in our menu. Contact us for specific allergen information.
Can I modify my order after placing it?
Order modifications are possible within 2 minutes of placement. After that, the kitchen begins preparation. Please call us immediately at +91 9876543210 for urgent changes.
How do coupon codes work?
Enter your coupon code at checkout. Available codes: BLAZE50 (₹50 off), FIRST100 (₹100 off first order), MIDNIGHT (₹80 off after 11PM), VIP200 (₹200 off). One code per order.
